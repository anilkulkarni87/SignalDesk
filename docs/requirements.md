# SignalDesk V1 Requirements

## Purpose

This document translates the NovaCart discovery findings and core workflows into testable system requirements for SignalDesk V1.

The requirements are intentionally capability-focused rather than technology-focused. They define what the system must do and the constraints under which it must operate without prescribing LLM, agent, retrieval, database, or application frameworks prematurely.

---

# Functional Requirements

## FR-01 — Retrieve the At-Risk Customer Population

**Workflow:** Investigate an at-risk customer

**Requirement:**  
SignalDesk must be able to receive or retrieve the customer population identified as at risk by NovaCart's existing upstream risk model.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- The system can ingest or retrieve a weekly at-risk customer population.
- Each customer in the population has a stable customer identifier.
- The system preserves the upstream risk-model output rather than recalculating churn risk in V1.
- Missing or invalid customer identifiers are surfaced explicitly.
- SignalDesk does not independently add customers to the at-risk population unless a future requirement explicitly introduces that capability.

---

## FR-02 — Retrieve Customer Evidence

**Workflow:** Investigate an at-risk customer

**Requirement:**  
SignalDesk must retrieve the customer evidence required to investigate an at-risk customer.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- Given a valid customer identifier, the system can retrieve available purchase history.
- The system can retrieve available behavioral engagement history.
- The system can retrieve available email engagement history.
- The system can retrieve available support history.
- Missing evidence is represented explicitly rather than fabricated.
- Evidence returned for one customer must not be mixed with evidence from another customer.

---

## FR-03 — Calculate Deterministic Customer Metrics

**Workflow:** Investigate an at-risk customer

**Requirement:**  
SignalDesk must calculate deterministic customer metrics required for investigation when those values can be derived from structured data.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- The system can calculate metrics such as purchase recency and purchase frequency from authoritative customer data.
- The system can calculate period-over-period changes when the necessary historical data is available.
- Derived values are reproducible from the underlying source records.
- Missing source data results in an explicit unavailable or incomplete value rather than an estimated fact.
- Deterministic metrics are not generated by probabilistic reasoning when they can be computed directly.

---

## FR-04 — Present an Evidence-Backed Investigation Summary

**Workflow:** Investigate an at-risk customer

**Requirement:**  
SignalDesk must present a concise investigation summary that helps the Retention Specialist understand the customer's recent behavior and the important signals associated with declining engagement.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- The summary identifies material changes in customer behavior.
- Material factual claims in the summary can be traced to supporting customer evidence.
- The summary distinguishes available facts from unavailable or incomplete information.
- The specialist can inspect the evidence supporting material findings.
- The summary does not introduce unsupported customer facts.

---

## FR-05 — Recommend an Intervention

**Workflow:** Determine the appropriate intervention

**Requirement:**  
SignalDesk must recommend one allowed intervention based on the available customer evidence.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- For an investigation with sufficient evidence, exactly one intervention is returned:
  - `NO_ACTION`
  - `RETENTION_OFFER`
  - `ESCALATE_TO_SUPPORT`
- The recommendation includes a concise rationale explaining why the intervention was selected.
- The recommendation identifies the material customer evidence supporting the decision.
- Supporting evidence must correspond to facts available in the investigation.
- Unsupported facts must not be introduced into the rationale.
- When available evidence is insufficient to make a defensible recommendation, the investigation is explicitly identified as incomplete rather than fabricating evidence.
- The recommended intervention must comply with applicable deterministic eligibility, consent, and business rules.

---

## FR-06 — Enforce Deterministic Business Rules

**Workflow:** Determine the appropriate intervention

**Requirement:**  
SignalDesk must enforce deterministic eligibility, consent, and intervention constraints before presenting a recommendation as valid.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- A retention offer cannot be presented as valid when the customer is ineligible.
- Consent restrictions are applied before an intervention is considered valid.
- Hard business constraints are evaluated independently from probabilistic recommendation logic.
- A recommendation cannot override a failed deterministic constraint.
- When a proposed action is blocked, the system records which rule prevented the action.
- Rule evaluation produces reproducible results for the same inputs and rule version.

---

## FR-07 — Human Review

**Workflow:** Review and approve a proposed intervention

**Requirement:**  
SignalDesk must allow a Retention Specialist to review and make the final decision on a proposed intervention.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- Given a proposed intervention, the specialist can select exactly one review outcome:
  - `APPROVE`
  - `MODIFY`
  - `REJECT`
- If the specialist selects `MODIFY`, they can choose a different allowed intervention:
  - `NO_ACTION`
  - `RETENTION_OFFER`
  - `ESCALATE_TO_SUPPORT`
- The system records the original recommendation and the specialist's final decision separately.
- The system records whether the recommendation was approved, modified, or rejected.
- The review outcome can later be used to calculate recommendation agreement and override rates.
- A recommendation alone does not automatically execute a consequential customer action.

---

## FR-08 — Record Investigation and Decision Outcomes

**Workflow:** Review and approve a proposed intervention

**Requirement:**  
SignalDesk must persist enough information about each completed investigation to support auditability, evaluation, and analytics.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- Each completed investigation is associated with a customer identifier.
- The system records the investigation timestamp.
- The system records the evidence considered.
- The system records the investigation summary.
- The system records the original recommendation.
- The system records the final human decision.
- The system records whether the recommendation was approved, modified, or rejected.
- Available specialist rationale is persisted without overwriting the original recommendation.
- Investigation records can be retrieved for later analysis.

---

## FR-09 — Support Escalation Handoff

**Workflow:** Handle a retention-driven support escalation

**Requirement:**  
When `ESCALATE_TO_SUPPORT` is the final decision, SignalDesk must create a context-rich handoff for the Customer Support Specialist.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- A support handoff is created only when the final human decision is `ESCALATE_TO_SUPPORT`.
- The handoff includes:
  - Customer identifier
  - Investigation summary
  - Material supporting evidence
  - Original SignalDesk recommendation
  - Final Retention Specialist decision
  - Reason for escalation
- The escalation context is persisted.
- The escalation context is accessible to the Customer Support Specialist.
- Support can distinguish the original SignalDesk recommendation from the final human-approved decision.
- The handoff contains enough context for Support to begin resolution without repeating the full retention investigation.
- The escalation event is recorded so Retention Managers can measure escalation volume and available outcomes.

---

## FR-10 — Manager Analytics

**Workflow:** Monitor retention investigation performance

**Requirement:**  
SignalDesk must provide Retention Managers with a separate analytics surface for monitoring the performance of the retention investigation workflow.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- The analytics surface shows weekly investigation coverage, including:
  - Total at-risk customers
  - Total completed investigations
  - Investigation coverage percentage
- The analytics surface shows weekly investigation-time metrics, including:
  - Median investigation time
  - p95 investigation time
  - Percentage of investigations completed within the target time
- The analytics surface shows final-intervention distribution:
  - `NO_ACTION`
  - `RETENTION_OFFER`
  - `ESCALATE_TO_SUPPORT`
- The analytics surface shows recommendation agreement metrics:
  - Percentage approved without modification
  - Percentage modified by specialists
  - Percentage rejected by specialists
- The analytics surface shows support-escalation volume for the selected reporting period.
- Metrics are calculated from persisted investigation and decision records rather than manually entered summary values.

# Non-Functional Requirements

## NFR-01 — Responsiveness

**Requirement:**  
SignalDesk must respond quickly enough to support an interactive customer-investigation workflow.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- Under the defined V1 test workload, at least 95% of valid investigation requests complete in less than 8 seconds.
- Test reporting includes at minimum p50 and p95 response time.
- Timed-out and failed requests are counted separately.
- System-response latency is measured separately from total human investigation time.

---

## NFR-02 — Evidence Traceability

**Requirement:**  
Material factual claims used in an investigation or recommendation must be traceable to supporting evidence.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- Each material factual claim has an identifiable supporting source.
- A claim is marked unsupported when no corresponding evidence exists.
- Evidence contradicting a claim causes the claim to fail evidence validation.
- Precise numerical claims must be derivable from the available evidence.
- Evaluation reporting can calculate the percentage of material claims supported by evidence.

---

## NFR-03 — Human Control of Consequential Actions

**Requirement:**  
SignalDesk V1 must preserve meaningful human control before consequential customer actions are executed.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- A recommendation does not automatically send a retention offer.
- A recommendation does not automatically create or execute a support action.
- The final intervention decision is captured from a Retention Specialist.
- Any future execution capability must use the recorded human-approved final decision rather than the original recommendation alone.
- The system can distinguish recommendation state from approved-action state.

---

## NFR-04 — Auditability

**Requirement:**  
Completed investigations must contain enough recorded information to reconstruct the decision process.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- Each completed investigation records the customer identifier.
- Each completed investigation records relevant timestamps.
- Evidence considered during the investigation is recorded or referenceable.
- The original recommendation is preserved.
- The final human decision is preserved.
- Recommendation and final decision cannot overwrite one another.
- A historical investigation can be retrieved for later review.

---

## NFR-05 — Deterministic Constraint Enforcement

**Requirement:**  
Eligibility, consent, and other hard business constraints must be enforced deterministically and must not be bypassable by the recommendation layer.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- The same rule inputs and rule version produce the same result.
- A failed hard constraint prevents an otherwise recommended intervention from being presented as valid.
- Constraint failures are visible to the Retention Specialist.
- The recommendation layer cannot override a deterministic rule result.
- Rule outcomes can be recorded for later audit and debugging.

# V1 Scope Guardrails

The requirements above do not introduce the following capabilities into V1:

- Replacing the existing at-risk model
- Automatically identifying a new churn population
- Automatically executing retention offers
- Automatically executing support actions
- Optimizing discount values
- Estimating causal treatment effects
- Claiming causal retention lift from synthetic data
- Requiring an LLM, vector database, agent framework, or orchestration framework

Technology choices should be introduced only when they are justified by these requirements and later implementation evidence.
