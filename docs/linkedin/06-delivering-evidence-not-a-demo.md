# Post 06 - Delivering Evidence, Not a Demo

After 17 technical milestones, the final SignalDesk milestone added no new AI
behavior.

It asked whether the system could support a defensible decision.

The FDE delivery pack connects:

```text
customer workflow
requirements
architecture and authority
evaluation evidence
security boundaries
deployment and operations
economics and limitations
next validation decision
```

The most important part was preserving what the repository did not prove.

SignalDesk did not measure real analyst investigation time. It did not establish
policy correctness with domain experts. It did not demonstrate production
reliability or customer impact. Its ROI model is arithmetic over fictional
assumptions, not realized savings.

What it did establish is narrower and useful:

- a repeatable synthetic data substrate;
- frozen evaluations for model and retrieval behavior;
- bounded tools and stateful workflows;
- exact-payload human approval;
- a usable local analyst and observability experience;
- tested behavior for named failures;
- a traceable account of design decisions and limitations.

The recommended next step is therefore not another framework. It is real
discovery, independent labeling, and a read-only shadow pilot with explicit stop
conditions.

That is the shift I wanted to learn: from building an AI artifact to reducing a
customer's decision uncertainty with evidence.
